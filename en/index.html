01 / The place

A row of white parasols, the pale wood of the beach cabins, a kitchen that cooks fish from the Adriatic. The rest is up to the day.

We are on the Levante beach, right by the pier. People come for the sea and stay for the table: breakfast from our bakery, lunch in the shade, an aperitivo as the light fades, dinner by candlelight.

07 / Good to know

Before you come.

Can’t find the answer? or write to us.

How does online booking work?

Choose lunch or dinner, the day, the number of guests and one of the available times. Leave your name, phone number and, if you like, an email and a note. When our staff confirm the request, or if they cannot accept it, we write to you by email or text message.

I can’t see any available times. Why?

We are closed that day, or that time is already fully booked. Try another time, another day, or call us: sometimes a table frees up.

When are you open?

Season dates, opening hours for each service and planned closures are in the Contact section and are updated whenever they change. Closed days cannot be selected in the booking calendar.

We are more than 12 people.

Online bookings are for tables of up to 12 people. For larger groups, company dinners or private parties write to info@mareconlaccento.it.

Can I book a parasol here?

No, the online form is for the restaurant only. For parasols, sunbeds and tents please ask at the beach directly.

I have an allergy or intolerance.

Mention it in the booking notes and remind our staff before ordering. Many dishes are available gluten free and the allergen sheet can be downloaded from the kitchen section.

Are you coming with small children?

Let us know in the notes and we will have a high chair ready. There is a playground for little ones on the beach.
